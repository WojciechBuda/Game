Stackville is a colourful block puzzle where every line you clear helps you build your very own town.

🧱 BLOCK PUZZLE
Move, rotate and drop falling blocks. Clear lines, chain combos and earn coins. Watch out for surprises: bombs, blasts that clear a whole row or column, tiny single and double blocks, and mushroom blocks!

🗺️ HUNDREDS OF LEVELS
Play through levels on the map, earn stars and unlock new challenges. Or play endless Arcade and beat your own best score.

🏙️ YOUR 3D TOWN
Spend what you earn on houses, shops, parks and towers. Upgraded buildings change shape and grow more elegant and grand. Need more room? Buy new islands joined by bridges, decorate them with fountains, statues and gazebos, or demolish and rebuild. Townsfolk give you random missions with rewards.

🌲 FOREST ISLAND & 🌻 GARDEN ISLAND
Pick mushrooms in every game mode, grow your forest island, plant flowers and vegetables in the garden and cook treats from your harvest.

🏃 3D RUN
Dash along colourful tracks, switch lanes, jump and grab coins. You have 3 lives – how far can you go?

🧗 TOWER CLIMB
Jump higher and higher from ledge to ledge – tap to jump, double tap for a double jump, slide to steer. Jungle, waterfalls, outer space and more.

🐾 YOUR PET
Feed it, wash it, play with it and put it to bed. A happy pet means more rewards.

🎡 LUCKY WHEEL, SEASON PASS & WARDROBE
A free daily spin, boosters, seasonal rewards and dozens of skins, themes and outfits for your block.

✨ FOR EVERYONE
• Play offline, no account needed
• Progress is saved on your phone
• 6 languages: English, Polish, German, Spanish, French, Portuguese
• Adjustable music and sound volume, vibration and more

The game is free to play. It contains ads and optional in-app purchases (such as ad-free Premium). Most items can also be earned just by playing.
